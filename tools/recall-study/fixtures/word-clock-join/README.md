# Word-clock join fixtures

All fixtures are synthetic. No participant transcript, gold, annotation or video
content is included.

`g1-historical.json` is copied byte-for-byte from the G1 producer artifact
`historical.json`: source `b7d908a8`, evidence `80d4fbd2`, SHA-256
`acea0831a78b03f5b7cfa548e81f037aa997d37dbdae8b6cdfd4b24ac82f59c7`.
The committed producer receipt records the same digest; `provenance.json` names
the Scala fixture and artifact writer. Its original text is "Anna arrived. Bob
left." and its inventory has four words and two units. The mapping record itself
is unchanged.

`words.csv` and `lineage.json` supply original, independently authored external
timing. An empty second record distinguishes CSV positions from parsed word
indices. The first OpenNeuro word onset is missing, while the last two are 10 and
9 seconds. `expected.json` contains hand-authored counts, positions, onsets and
summary witnesses; it was not generated by the reader.

`unicode.csv` represents `😀 same same z\nq <NBSP>`, with an empty record after
the emoji. Its authored UTF-16 spans are `[0,2)`, `[3,7)`, `[8,12)`, `[13,16)` and
`[17,18)`. A unit named `u😀` owns the first and third words through two separate
components, leaving the intervening repeated word unassigned. A `[13,14)` unit
overlaps the fourth word without containing it. A `[5,5)` empty unit lies inside
the unassigned second word and must own nothing. The final NBSP word remains
because Java trim does not remove it.

`unicode-inventory-envelope.json` and `regrouped-inventory-envelope.json` are
authored inventory-only envelopes using the G1 wire shape. Their digests were
constructed independently with a UTF-16LE/unsigned-short reference calculation,
not by importing the reader. They are **not complete checked mapping results**;
their declared record digest is a zero sentinel. The reader's explicit
inventory-only validation boundary is essential. The regrouped fixture retains
the original four word IDs but places them in one new unit, changing segmentation
and inventory identities without changing the words or transferred onsets.

Neither source pairing nor an exact structural join establishes a shared real
recording. The duplicate-transcript timing test deliberately changes timing
bytes while preserving text; both correspondences succeed with distinct source
identities and an unverified recording binding.
