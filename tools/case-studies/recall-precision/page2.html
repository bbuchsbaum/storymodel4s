Each grey block is one hypothesis for one recall reference: horizontally the stretch of speech, vertically the film content it points to, and darkness is its probability (overlapping alternatives do not add up to a confident one; they are drawn side by side in probability). Orange outlines are the human scene coding; small ticks under the map mark its scene changes. Gaps in the tracks are silence or talk that is not about the film, never zero.
