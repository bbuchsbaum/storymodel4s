# Vision

storymodel4s aims to make **Narrative Process Alignment** an inspectable,
measurable scientific object.

A story, once encountered, becomes a process in a mind. Recall is not a copy of
that process. It is another process: selective, compressed, reordered,
revisited, reinterpreted, and sometimes supplemented from outside. Narrative
Process Alignment represents the transformation between the two as a partial,
probabilistic, hierarchical, open-world, and approximately relation-preserving
mapping.

Researchers should be able to move between the words of a transcript, the
propositions expressed by those words, the events and states that make up a
story, and the route a person takes while recalling it. A scene change,
flashback, causal link, vague summary, precise detail, blended memory, or
external association should not disappear into one embedding or one score.
Each should remain visible in the part of the model that can represent it
faithfully, and its role in the transformation should be measurable.

The observational coordinate system for each narrative process is its exact
surface text. Every token, clause, proposition, event, scene, episode, boundary
signal, and feature estimate points back to textual evidence in its source or
transcript. Word-level measurements such as imageability can form aligned
feature tracks, be reduced over declared windows, contribute evidence to scene
or episode boundaries, and still be inspected independently of the hierarchy
they helped infer. Higher-order interpretations never erase the observations
from which they were built.

Such a system must produce its representations unattended and remain auditable;
the mission defines how.

This foundation should let researchers ask:

- Which details and gists survived, with which roles, polarity, contexts,
  chronology, causality, sensory content, and discourse path intact?
- Did recall follow presentation order, story-world time, causal structure, or
  another route involving jumps, reversals, revisits, and compression?
- How do local linguistic and continuous feature signals accumulate into
  scenes, episodes, goals, and other narrative structures?
- When is constructive content a source-consistent inference, association,
  commentary, or unsupported intrusion?
- For an Autobiographical Interview, what target episode is implied by the
  account, and what can be said about its organization without claiming
  historical truth or genuine re-experiencing?
- How do conclusions change when familiar totals, population analyses, or
  neural regressors carry the underlying alignment uncertainty forward?

Success is not a machine that announces the correct interpretation of a story
or a single answer to "how well was this remembered?" Success is a system that
makes its interpretation falsifiable and examinable: what survived, at what
grain and in what order; which roles, facts, relations, and contexts remained
intact; what was added from outside; which words support each conclusion; and
what alternatives remain under another model or policy.
