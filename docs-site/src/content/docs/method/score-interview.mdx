The Autobiographical Interview asks a person to describe a specific event. storymodel4s represents
the transcript as recall units and detail atoms, assigns each detail a probability distribution over
memory addresses, and derives two reader-facing results:

- traditional internal and external category counts; and
- a profile of event purity, density, perceptual content, probe gain, and related measures.

The public `BirthdayInterview` fixture is synthetic. Its participant recalls a fortieth-birthday
dinner, a rainy evening, a habitual family practice, a previous trip, and a cake brought out by a
waiter. The fixture also includes general and specific probes.

## Counts, resolution, and profile

<InterviewScoreFigure />

The model contains 18 participant recall units, 49 details, and 49 assessments.

### Traditional categories

Each category row contains three values:

- `point` is the expected fractional count under the address distribution;
- the interval's lower bound counts details whose modal address belongs to the category;
- the interval's upper bound counts details that assign any positive mass to the category; and
- `hard` is the rounded whole-number count under the standard policy.

The address masses in this fixture are uncalibrated. These intervals bound model-routed expected
counts; they are not statistical confidence or credible intervals.

### Two kinds of coverage

Observed coverage is `49/49`: every detail has an observed count mass. Placement resolution answers
a different question. The model resolved `0.6876` of the placement mass and left `0.3124` unresolved.
It excluded no mass under the scoring policy used here.

The conditional internal ratio is approximately `0.4898`. The resolved fraction clears the policy's
`0.50` threshold, so the ratio is available. Unresolved mass enters neither the internal numerator
nor the external denominator.

### The richer profile

Event purity is `0.8750`, and probe gain is `0.4286`. Episodic density is reported per participant
word and per second. The target-supported perceptual profile contains visual and olfactory detail.
The mental-state profile is empty. The fixture records embarrassment as a detail, but its placement
remains unresolved and therefore supplies no target-supported mental channel.

## Scope of the fixture

`BirthdayInterview.model` is a deterministic demonstration entry point. The current induction rules
operate on this synthetic transcript and use uncalibrated scores. The result characterizes the event
implied by the account; it establishes no historical truth or genuine re-experiencing.
