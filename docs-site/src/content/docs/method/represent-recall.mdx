`RecallSegmenter.segment` supplies a reproducible baseline for turning a transcript into a checked
`RecallGraph`. It chooses unit boundaries with fixed heuristics and creates shallow proposition
sketches. The graph contains the transcript, its source atlas, ordered units, recalled entities,
coreference links, and explicit temporal or causal relations.

This guide uses the same recall as the alignment guide:

> One of the men with him got hit by an arrow and they lifted him into the boat.

## Read this as one analysis

The baseline splits the sentence at the conjunction into two units:

| Unit | Span | Text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `[0, 43)` | `One of the men with him got hit by an arrow` |
| 1 | `[48, 78)` | `they lifted him into the boat.` |

Each unit keeps the exact transcript span that supports it. The `Checked` type parameter records
structural facts: the ordinals, spans, relation endpoints, entity references, and text-at-span checks
all passed validation. Choosing the best boundaries and proposition analyses remains a separate
inference.

Both units are episodic assertions with unmarked uncertainty, positive polarity, and asserted
modality. The first sketch records predicate `get` with agent `men`. The second records predicate
`lift`, agent `they`, and patient `him`.

The segmenter also creates an entity named `men` and one coreference link. That link connects a later
pronoun with the earlier plural mention. Recall order remains available through unit ordinals and
`recall.chain`; the transcript states no explicit temporal or causal connective, so both typed
relation collections are empty.

## Carry uncertainty into research results

The shallow proposition sketch reads “got hit” as predicate `get` and records only one participant
for that unit. Another reasonable parser could choose a different predicate or a different boundary.

Treat this graph as a segmentation-conditioned heuristic baseline. A terminal research result needs
a named segmentation policy or receipt and must retain plausible segmentation and interpretation
alternatives. This example stops at construction and structural validation.
