The library's value lies less in a list of types than in the distinctions those types refuse to
collapse.

## Surface evidence is the anchor

Source and transcript text live on explicit surface axes. Claims cite exact span sets; an inference
cites upstream claims. Evidence is not a comment attached after the model is built—it is part of
the accepted claim.

Offsets are UTF-16 because they must round-trip with common text and browser systems. Surface time
or position is not assumed to be story-world time.

## A context is not optional metadata

Every situation lives in a context. The root is a **NarratedWorld**, not an assertion about
objective reality. Speech, belief, intention, and nested reports stay within their own frames
unless evidence licenses a relationship to the root.

This is what prevents “the warriors said he was shot” from silently becoming “he was shot.”

## Three clocks remain three clocks

- **Discourse order**: where content appears in the telling.
- **Story-world time**: when represented events occur.
- **Recall time**: the order in which a person produces recall units.

A retelling can mention an earlier event after a later one. A recall can jump backward or revisit a
scene. Collapsing these axes makes genuine reorderings disappear.

## Partial alignment includes elsewhere

A recall need not map neatly onto one source event. It may omit, merge, split, reorder, elaborate,
or add content. Alignment therefore distributes mass over source nodes **and explicit external
states** such as association or task commentary. External mass is a modeled destination, not error
residue.

## Fidelity is faceted

An event can be recalled while one aspect is wrong. Actor, object, action, location, context,
polarity, modality, and role structure can be assessed separately. A role-reversed recall should
still anchor to the event it distorts; it should not be counted as faithful or discarded as a pure
intrusion.

## Missing support stays missing

Coverage travels with estimates. Missing evidence does not silently become zero, one, or a neutral
constant, and downstream normalization must not redistribute unsupported mass without naming a
different estimand.

## Embeddings nominate; they do not decide

The portable embedding contract includes deterministic hashed n-gram and TF-IDF baselines. A local
ONNX sentence-encoder adapter and a structural graph-kernel adapter also exist in JVM-only modules.
All of them are feature views. No vector participates in model identity, equality, graph structure,
or claim acceptance.
