The root-directed signature inventory is an audit population, not a list of checked values.
The corrected walk follows public enum/sealed alternatives, constructors and result-producing
companion methods. This exposes more dependencies than the old 781-symbol inventory. The old
`semantic-inventory.json` is retained as adverse history. The reviewed corrected population is
2,058 symbols and 21,499 edges at source `2c4b74f9`, retained in
`semantic-inventory-repaired-2c4b74f9.json.gz` and its named provenance sidecar. A later
exact-distribution refresh is recorded separately. Public signatures do not prove factory behavior, execution or calibration.

family decision in `docs/api-stability.md` remains the intended boundary; its final freeze requires

## Additional family judgments from the complete coverage crosswalk

These dispositions distinguish the constructor promise from the containing API's eventual
stability classification. The named suites passed in the exact `eb48a562` full gate. This is
an evidence crosswalk, not a fresh mutation campaign or certification of every reachable class.

### A1. Claims, credence, provenance and the historical claim ledger

`ClaimMeta.of` checks its narrow status/span-evidence relation; `Credence` checks finite raw
scores and the prerequisites for a calibrated basis. Neither checks that an asserted claim is
true or reruns a supplied calibration model. `Evidence`, `Resolved`, `Provenance` and
`BuildReceipt` are descriptive payloads. `ProviderCall` protects the typed nonblank prompt-version
boundary through its explicit constructors, while its provider/input/output declarations are not
an authenticated invocation. `ConstructionProbeSuite` and the retained external probe distinguish
these guarantees.

`ClaimLedger` has a known generated `Mirror`/`fromProduct` construction opening. The historical
census `bd-01M183VBPNEAPT5JNQMBMYMKQ9` is closed; the bounded repair is now tracked by
`bd-01M4XZ2H3XHGY8AQ80W07PR8M0`. Both the independent reviewer and primary agent compiled and ran
the same discriminating external probe. A hidden map entry can have `size=0`, `all=empty` and a
successful `lookup`; `JsonLines.claims` then writes zero bytes and loses the entry on reload.
Duplicate order serializes duplicate claims that the reader refuses, and a miskeyed entry changes
lookup key on roundtrip. Direct new/apply/copy refuse. Normal `empty.add`/`addAll`, duplicate
refusal and codec roundtrip controls pass. The normal model and reader producers use that builder
route; the probe does not establish occurrence in the current study data. This blocks a universal
stable checked-ledger guarantee and remains an explicit follow-up for the demonstrated study path.

The [probe assessment](claim-ledger-probe/assessment.json) binds exact source, controls and
consumers; [independent output](claim-ledger-probe/run.log) and
[primary reproduction](claim-ledger-probe/parent-run.log) retain the observed consequence.

### A2. Text identity, spans and proposal atlases

`TextSpan`, `TokenRange`, `SpanSet` and `StorySource` have checked local range, normalization
or content/checksum construction. `SpanRef`, `SurfaceUnit`, kinds and supplied metadata remain
open descriptors: a unit identifier in a span is not proof of membership in an atlas.
`SurfaceAtlas.of` checks unique units, range/order and parent relationships against its source;
`TextNarrativeAtlas.of` derives the source bundle and supports. `AnchoredNarrativeAtlas.of`
checks the playback-axis kind, distinct proposal/surface identities, sentence membership and
support on the exact bundle. `BoundProposalSurface` derives the surface/receipt association;
it explicitly does not attest that the receipt's producer generated those bytes. `StoryTitle`
checks title shape and preserves caller-supplied provenance, not title truth or attribution.

Evidence: `coreprobe.ConstructionProbeSuite` names the negative-span, inverted-token-range,
sorted/deduplicated-span and source-content construction doors; `AtlasConstructionBoundarySuite`,
`D1aConstructionBoundarySuite`, `AtlasSuite` and `D1aAtlasSuite` cover atlas/proposal admission.
Opaque IDs/checksums remain aliases with their own checked parsers; a lawful identifier is not
proof that the named observation exists. `NarrativeSourceAtlas` is sealed to its checked text and
anchored alternatives; `D1aConstructionBoundarySuite` refuses external extension. The separate
`SourceView` interface remains an open extension contract.

### A3. Source descriptors, content projections and feature sidecars

`SourceView`, participant summaries, source references, context/relation tags, scoring positions,
analysis/target grain requests and `SegmentEvidence` are descriptive input contracts. `NodeSummary`
has explicit public rebuilding with a checked `ImportanceWeight`; it does not independently
validate every parent, level or semantic assertion. `SourceRepresentation` performs the source,
axis and target joins described above. The restricted `ParticipantContent`, `UnitContent`,
`TargetContent`, `Members` and `ContentGrain` are outputs of the canonical/historical content
projection routes; they are not a new public assertion of semantic truth. `SemanticOutcomeSummary`
is counted from outcomes. Their eventual stable exposure remains a dependency decision.

`FeatureSpace`, `FeatureValueSchema`, `FeatureTarget`, `DurationEstimate`, `TemporalHypothesis`
and `WorldTimeTransition` are open descriptions/hypotheses. For example, a schema's vector
dimension or a duration estimate is not a validated numeric observation merely by construction.
`SidecarManifest.of` checks positive dimension, nonnegative rows, layout and nonoverflowing byte
length. `FeatureRef.of` checks a nonnegative row; `FeatureRef.validated` separately checks the
manifest's space and row range. A manifest checksum is a declaration until actual bytes are
verified. `SidecarBlockRows.from` checks positive block size. No generic feature descriptor is
promoted to a checked measurement or stable builder by being reachable.

Evidence: the named `SidecarManifest`/`FeatureRef` checks in
`featuresprobe.ConstructionBoundarySuite`, sidecar geometry/range tests and `codec.SidecarSuite`
cover those narrow promises; that suite also retains unrelated known-open feature constructors,
which must not be mistaken for passing checked-boundary claims. Source/importance/content courts
and `SourceRepresentationSuite` cover the relevant joins, unavailable defaults and content order.

### A4. Contextual codec inputs and admitted/exported wrappers

`ExpectedMappingContext` and `DerivationContext` are honest public request products. A caller
can combine unrelated individually valid values; the decoder must recheck their relation.
`MappingCodecSuite` distinguishes each member's context, declared twins, foreign inventory,
missing derivation context, changed source bodies and asserted provenance. Context construction
itself grants no execution authority. `BoundCaptionSourceCodec.Imported` and
`MediaCaptionSourceCodec.Imported` are restricted reader outputs with checked source/caption
bindings; their boundary suites cover external construction. `TextSourceArtifactCodec.Bundle`
and `MappingExchange.Bundle` hold the files produced by their restricted writers. An in-memory
bundle neither proves files were written nor supplies disclosure permission. Reader/permission
and independent exchange checks remain separate evidence for actual output.

### A5. Executed temporal query and projection values

`TemporalSupport` and its coordinates/nodes derive exact selected-axis support from the supplied
source. `TemporalQuery.Region`, prepared domains, ledgers, bounds/readouts and `TemporalGrid`
are checked producer values. Their request declarations, allocation choices and selection tags
remain public inputs. `TemporalProjection.compile` validates bins, finite lawful mixtures,
unique/known declarations and any recall window before constructing every inventory row; a failed
or unavailable row stays represented. `TemporalExposure.checked` binds recall timing, processing
and inventory; readouts preserve the overlap rule and exact rational-clock window. Foreign clocks,
overlap, out-of-range requests and missing measures retain their refusals/unavailable states.

Evidence: `TemporalSupportSuite`, `TemporalQuerySuite`, `TemporalProjectionCodecSuite`, and
outside-package `TemporalGridBoundarySuite`, `TemporalProjectionBoundarySuite` and
`TemporalExposureBoundarySuite`. Named tests retain disconnected support, half-open atoms,
large ticks, external/omitted mass and exposure overflow. These contracts supply model-conditional
allocation and declared-time exposure; they do not recover physical synchronization, a joint path
posterior or clock uncertainty. The separately audited imported inspection surface keeps its own
artifact identity and authority boundaries.

### A6. Exposed implementation dependencies awaiting boundary disposition

The complete crosswalk explicitly retains the provider/vector/privacy, inference/configuration,
orchestration/preparation/disclosure, media and corpus dependency families. These 169 nominal
entries in the current semantic population have no blanket checked or stable disposition. The
narrative implementation family likewise retains its experimental classification despite its
association with the broader graph row. The accepted freeze rule requires either promotion and
specific constructor judgment of retained signatures, or a supported facade that no longer exposes
them. A registered execution, a checksum-verified artifact, a caller-declared provider receipt and
a proposed configuration are different guarantees. The current strict-registration row covers its
named registrations, not every dependency. This is the remaining concrete owner boundary decision;
no selected implementation is silently excluded from the inventory.

## Release consequence

The ordinary study path has independent execution and readback evidence. The pre-freeze audit
still has a concrete boundary decision: `LocalEvidence.candidates` exposes `CandidateSet`, and
public result/factory signatures reach configuration/provider and narrative implementation types
previously described as experimental. The owner-approved rule requires promotion and audit of
those exact dependencies, or a narrower supported facade before the final freeze. A zero-length
`unresolved` list only means the walker resolved its symbols; it is not acceptance of that decision.

The candidate/truncation probes are retained under `construction-probes/`; the additional
known ledger opening and its serialization consequence are retained under `claim-ledger-probe/`. Their findings are
follow-ups for the currently qualified study/inspection path and blockers for the broader claims
identified in the table. Keep them on their own Mote records; do not close the final release gate
or publish `1.0.0` on the basis of enumeration, the compatibility rehearsal or a passing build.
