## Decision and existing exposure

The question is whether automatic reconstruction improves event localization on new authorized
material without hiding failed observations, trading accuracy for selective coverage, or
turning identity ambiguity into spurious organization differences. Conditional oracle identity
is a diagnostic arm only. Its gain cannot establish an automatic repair's efficacy.

The [metadata audit](evidence/prospective-evaluation-20261015/exposure-metadata.json) binds the
committed split/read-ledger hashes. Friends has 13 development and 10 test participants;
Memento has 60 development and 63 test participants. Both ledgers record zero final openings
and zero count-only reads. Both admissions describe these splits as **model-untouched, not
unseen**. Ledger silence cannot prove that all historical access used the guard, nor exclude
training exposure. Their current final-opening restrictions continue to govern.

The organization challenge report explicitly records that its holdout was opened. Existing
organization, identity-hypothesis and Sherlock/FilmFestival development results are reusable
for development and implementation checks, not an untouched confirmatory evaluation. A new
filename, perturbation or participant split cannot remove exposure of the underlying source.

## Two distinct experiments

**Controlled recovery** tests a known simulation mechanism at fixed true organization. Freeze
a generator, its scenario-family inventory and seed partition before producing evaluation
outputs. Development and evaluation families must differ in underlying event/identity templates,
not only names or surface wording. Cross name ambiguity, repeated identities/events, omission,
paraphrase, transcription degradation, reversals/revisits, missing observations and external
content. Keep the true organization and complete gold correspondence explicit. Evaluate both
clean and degraded versions of each independent generated family. This can establish recovery
under that generator, not empirical accuracy on human recall or neural validity.

**Blinded human reference comparison** uses only newly approved material and actual coder
availability. Identify source/stimulus families and participants before allocation; all versions
of a source and all sessions of a participant stay on the same side. If source families cannot
be held out, label the result as participant transfer conditional on the fixed sources. Coders
receive approved source/reference material and unit definitions but neither model outputs nor
the first coding. Resolve disagreements by the predeclared procedure with uncertainty retained.
Reference agreement is reported separately; it is not automatically truth or a human ceiling.

Only metadata and eligibility manifests are prepared before approval. Participant recall and
gold stay in the authorized environment; no text enters Git under this protocol.

## Arms and estimand to freeze

Use exactly three achieved arms on matched inputs: lexical baseline, current pinned local
neural reference, and the declared automatic reconstruction. An optional oracle-identity arm
is labelled diagnostic everywhere. No model search, temperature fit, eligibility revision or
candidate selection uses evaluation outcomes. All fitting and threshold choice uses development
only. The preregistration must bind code, model/tokenizer bytes, encoder fingerprint, scoring
configuration, segmentation, candidate policy, reconstruction settings and development exposure.
Retain failure and input receipts for every arm. Frozen settings must be executable, not prose
such as "best current model".

The proposed single primary empirical contrast is reconstruction minus the local neural
reference in **participant-average correct event assignments over all eligible gold-bearing
units**. A failed run, abstention or omitted output is incorrect for this denominator, not
silently removed. Freeze how set-valued/uncertain gold contributes before scoring. Do not
replace this contrast with pooled-unit accuracy, oracle-identity accuracy or accuracy restricted
to emitted assignments. The lexical contrast and per-condition metrics are secondary; do not
promote them to a new primary after seeing results.

Report candidate inclusion, event localization, identity selection, specificity, emitted-decision
risk/coverage and organization error separately. A candidate-retrieval gain is not an event
localization gain. Use the actual emitted decision for confidence/risk, after the existing
instrument repair is independently qualified. Raw scores are not calibrated probabilities;
probability calibration needs its own declared measure and disjoint eligible calibration data.

## Organization and missingness

At fixed true organization, report paired clean/degraded error and recovered backward, forward,
same-target and external transitions for each independent scenario family. Never form a transition
across an unavailable, omitted or failed row. Counts use the declared inference unit, not expanded
display words. Quantify degradation-induced group differences with equal family weighting; keep
event discrimination and identity repair factors distinguishable in the crossed design.

For human material, freeze the organization estimand and reference construction separately from
event accuracy. Preserve unavailable values and their causes; do not silently replace missingness
with zero or condition all summaries on success. Retain complete observation and failure ledgers
so error, coverage and exclusion patterns can be examined against participant/stimulus properties.

## Required numerical and resource decisions before freeze

The present inventory does **not** establish enough fresh eligible units, coder time or a
scientifically defensible precision target. Thus no confirmatory sample size, success threshold
or launch is claimed. E1 remains open until the following executable fields are supplied and
independently approved before outcomes are read:

| Field | Required predeclared value and rationale |
| --- | --- |
| Population and inventory | Approved corpus/task, exact eligible source families and participants, development/evaluation memberships, prior exposure and deduplication evidence. |
| Precision and size | Independent-unit counts, fixed maximum recruitment/coding budget, minimally useful primary effect and justified interval-width target. Account for source and participant dependence; more rows do not manufacture independent participants. |
| Primary success | Numerical minimum improvement, uncertainty criterion and direction for the one declared contrast, plus a numerical coverage non-inferiority margin. |
| Futility | Numerical effect/precision criterion that yields negative or inconclusive status; fixed sample stopping rule with no unregistered interim peeking or optional extension. |
| Recovery guards | Numerical maximum organization error, tolerated degradation-induced group difference and minimum transition recovery, on declared scales/denominators. |
| Uncertainty | Exact cluster/resampling unit, handling of crossed sources/participants, interval algorithm, seeds and finite-sample sensitivity; distinguish fixed-source from source-general claims. |
| Secondary inference | Descriptive-only designation or exact multiplicity procedure; no secondary rescue of a failed primary. |
| Artifacts | Immutable executable pipeline/configuration and model/tokenizer hashes for every arm, with no placeholders or path-dependent defaults. |

Choosing these values after reading new outcomes invalidates confirmation. If resources cannot
support the precision target, register the smaller study as exploratory and retain the missing
resource explicitly. The requirement for numbers is not satisfied by inventing convenient
thresholds from already opened organization results.
